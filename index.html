Why some waves bend around a hill — and some can't

Radio, microwave and infrared are all electromagnetic waves — the difference is wavelength. That decides whether a signal can broadcast to many devices at once and bend around obstacles, or needs a transmitter and receiver staring straight at each other within range. Drag things around to find out.

Radio waves

  • Spreads in (almost) every direction from a single tower to many receivers at once

  • Used for AM/FM broadcast, Wi-Fi, walkie-talkies, marine radio
